Reading the data

Large speculators are the Legacy report’s non-commercial traders. Small speculators are non-reportable positions, not a precise count of retail traders. Commercials are traders classified as commercial by the CFTC.

COT index shows where each group’s net positioning sits within its rolling lookback: 0% is the lowest and 100% the highest. It does not show the percentage of traders who are long or short. Weekly changes are index points. A flat lookback has no index value.

Net positive means more longs than shorts; net negative means more shorts than longs. Rising net positioning can come from added longs, reduced shorts, or both. Changes describe positioning, not a trading signal.

One market at a time. These are CFTC contract-market aggregates across maturities, not an individual futures expiry. Sector labels are convenience classifications. This dashboard uses Legacy Futures Only throughout.
